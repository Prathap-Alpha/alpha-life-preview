Frequently asked questions.

Which currencies can I choose?

The concept offers five currency choices: US Dollars (USD), Euro (EUR), British Pounds (GBP), South African Rand (ZAR) and Botswana Pula (BWP). Final product availability and policy terms must be confirmed.

What does AI-first mean?

The vision is to use technology to simplify routine insurance steps, while keeping clear communication and a human purpose at the centre.

Does the proposed limit mean free insurance?

No. US$100,000 is a proposed free-cover underwriting limit for review. It is not a premium-free benefit, and the final terms and eligibility remain subject to approval.

Can I buy a policy on this page?

Not yet. This is a client-review concept, not a live policy offer. No cover is bound, no premiums are collected, and no application is submitted here.
